Coffee is a brewed drink prepared from roasted coffee beans, which are the seeds of berries from the Coffea plant. The two most commonly grown coffee beans are arabica and robusta. Once ripe, coffee berries are picked, processed, dried, roasted, ground, and brewed with near-boiling water to produce coffee as a beverage.
